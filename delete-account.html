Privacy policy

Request deletion without the app

You do not need to reinstall Forma to request account deletion. Email fawkesthepheonix240@gmail.com with the subject Forma account and data deletion.

  1. Send the request from the Google email address you use to sign in to Forma, if possible.
  2. State whether you want to delete your entire account and associated data or identify specific data you want removed.
  3. If you own any businesses, tell us whether you want to delete them or transfer them to another owner first.

We may ask for information needed to verify account ownership and clarify the scope of the request. Do not send your password, payment-card information, or sign-in codes.

Delete from inside Forma

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again. Before deleting the account, transfer ownership of or delete all businesses you own. For a business deletion, use the business's confirmed deletion action.

What deletion covers

In-app account deletion removes the sign-in account, account profile and access references, personal reminder data, and registered reminder devices stored beneath the account profile. Business deletion starts background removal of that business and its associated stored content. Ask support to review and delete associated account metadata not removed by the in-app flow, including applicable usage, operation, or billing records.

Business records that were transferred to another owner or that another business controls can remain with that business. Exported files and copies held by other people are not remotely erased. Backups, security logs, and records needed for legal, security, or transaction obligations may remain subject to applicable retention requirements; deletion is not guaranteed to be immediate across all provider backups.

Subscriptions: deleting your Forma account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel or manage any subscription in Google Play to prevent future renewal charges.

Questions about deletion can be sent to the same support email. See the Forma privacy policy for more about data processing.
